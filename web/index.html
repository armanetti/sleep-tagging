Tracking sleep phases through EEG networks

Each sleep stage is shown at the frequencies where its own spectrum peaks above the aperiodic background. Every node is an electrode, sized by the power inside that peak relative to the neighbouring sidebands. Every edge joins a pair of electrodes whose covariance at that rhythm is both strong and in phase, above the 95th percentile. The head is seen from above with the nose at the top: this is scalp topography, not a reconstruction of the brain underneath.
